# Phase02 physical tracking trial

Use only the new Phase02 artifact whose path/hash appears in the trial record. Native normal output is fake, voice off, canvas unconfirmed. No strokes or unrestricted drawing are needed. Prior Phase01 evidence is not reused for these gates.

1. Quit the earlier Steady copy. Open the new packaged app and Settings. Choose Head and the head-facing camera. Start camera probe; allow Camera when macOS asks. Check the mirrored preview on the panel and confirm that one head follows your comfortable movement. Record actual camera/format/delegate and camera placement.
2. Begin five-position calibration. Settle at neutral, press Capture / Retry, hold for two seconds. Repeat comfortable left, right, up and down, moving slowly between positions. A failed capture must be retried or cancelled; record the reason. Confirm calibration saved with default gain1/smoothing50. Move to the five positions and observe visual mapping. No native movement should occur.
3. Settle at a different comfortable neutral and press Recenter neutral; hold two seconds. Verify center moves, extents remain unchanged and input is paused. Start the 10s warmup+60s baseline and continue comfortable tracking, with Settings closed and the panel collapsed/unfocused for at least30seconds. Reopen Settings and report completion and observed tracking/preview behavior.
4. Stop capture, aim a camera toward a relaxed palm on the desk (same sensor in a different placement is permitted, identify it), choose Palm, start. Exactly one hand must be visible. Record selected model label. Complete all five calibration captures, check mapping and recenter. Run the same baseline/background check.
5. During Palm tracking, remove the hand for at least one second. Return it: mapping must stay blocked until Confirm selected hand. Verify that confirmation accepts only a fresh single matching-label candidate. Show two hands, then the opposite label alone: neither may take over. After loss, return the selected hand and explicitly confirm. Try one abrupt movement; record actual jump rejection or state no observation if not induced reliably.
6. Switch camera/mode away and back: saved calibration must match its camera/mode, remain paused and require canvas confirmation. Restart the same artifact once to verify settings persist, no camera/mic/pen starts automatically, and saved Palm selection requires confirmation after restart.

Report observed results, failed/retried captures, discomfort/precision/fatigue/accidental actions, camera placement/label, operator role (developer or intended-use artist), machine and Krita versions. Numeric summaries are collected separately; they do not establish physical landmark accuracy. No frames, recordings or biometric identity claims are retained.
