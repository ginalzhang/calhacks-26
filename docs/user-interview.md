# User interview: one real user, five questions

Goal: one honest sentence from a person in our community (students and adults with arthritis or essential tremor who still want to sketch and take their own notes) that we can show on the first demo screen, in the README and in the pitch. Ten minutes, in person or by video call. Show the prototype if it is running; otherwise show the 90 s fallback video.

Ground rules: don't ask about diagnoses, medications or history. Ask about what the hand does and what gets in the way. Write down their exact words for question 5. Nothing the app shows ever names a condition, so the quote must not either; trim it to one sentence and remove anything identifying.

## Consent (read aloud, word for word)

> "We're building a hackathon prototype that lets people draw and take notes without holding a pen. Can we ask you five questions and quote one sentence of your answer, without your name, in our demo and write-up? You can skip any question or stop at any time."

Record: consent given yes / no, date, interviewer initials. No names in this file.

## Questions

1. When you need to jot something down or sketch a quick diagram, what do you do today? What's the hardest part?
2. Which hand movements hurt or are hard to control: gripping, pinching, holding still, or reaching? Does that change during the day?
3. Have you used voice dictation, a stylus stabiliser, or head/eye tracking? What made you keep using it, or stop?
4. *(Show the prototype or the fallback video.)* If you could use this tomorrow, where would it get in your way?
5. Finish this sentence: "I'd use this if…"
